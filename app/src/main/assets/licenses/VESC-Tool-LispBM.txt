LispBM examples and editor symbol list

assets/lisp/examples/*.lbm are the example scripts bundled with VESC Tool
(res/LispBM/Examples/), and assets/lisp/symbols.txt lists the names from VESC
Tool's QCodeEditor/resources/languages/lisp.xml. Both are taken unchanged
(apart from the format of the symbol list) from https://github.com/vedderb/vesc_tool
at commit f1e15cb (2026-10-15).
